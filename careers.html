Join Our Team

Help us shape the future of autonomous transportation.

Why Join Us

We're building a ride-hailing platform designed specifically for self-driving electric vehicles. Our goal is to make autonomous mobility more accessible and efficient through intelligent ride matching, battery-aware dispatching, and effective fleet management.

As we develop our initial prototype, we're looking for people who can bring new perspectives and help turn our technology into a sustainable business.

Open Position

Business Development & Partnerships Associate

🏢 Business Development ⏱ Full-Time 📍 Location: To Be Determined

About the Role

We are seeking a Business Development & Partnerships Associate to support our company's growth and long-term business strategy. This role will focus on identifying market opportunities, developing relationships with potential partners, and helping establish a foundation for future commercial deployment.

Responsibilities

  • Research potential markets and identify opportunities for autonomous ride-hailing services.
  • Develop and maintain relationships with potential business partners, including autonomous vehicle providers and fleet operators.
  • Analyze industry trends, competitors, and customer needs to support business decisions.
  • Assist in developing business proposals, partnership strategies, and market expansion plans.
  • Collaborate with the engineering team to align product development with business and customer requirements.
  • Support investor presentations, fundraising preparation, and other business development activities.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Economics, or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Strong communication, research, and analytical skills.
  • Ability to build professional relationships and communicate technical concepts to nontechnical audiences.
  • Interest in autonomous vehicles, electric transportation, and emerging technologies.
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to work in a collaborative startup environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in business development, partnerships, or market research.
  • Familiarity with the transportation, automotive, or mobility technology industry.
  • Experience preparing business proposals or conducting competitive analysis.

Why Join Us?

You'll have the opportunity to contribute to an early-stage technology project exploring the future of autonomous mobility. Working alongside our founding engineering team, you'll help shape our business strategy, explore potential partnerships, and support the transition from a simulated prototype toward future commercial opportunities.

Compensation
Compensation and any potential equity participation will be determined based on the company's funding, hiring plans, and ownership structure.

Hiring Rationale

Our founding team consists of four software engineers with experience in technical development, project coordination, budgeting, and market research. While these skills provide a foundation for building our platform, we recognize the need for dedicated expertise in business development and external partnerships.

A Business Development & Partnerships Associate would help us establish relationships with autonomous vehicle manufacturers, fleet operators, and other potential partners. This role would also support market expansion planning, commercial strategy, and future fundraising efforts — allowing our engineering team to remain focused on product development.

Who We Are

Built for the next era of transportation

AutonomousGo is an autonomous mobility technology company developing a ride-hailing platform built specifically for self-driving electric vehicles. Inspired by the growing possibilities of autonomous transportation, we aim to make ride-booking more convenient, efficient, and reliable through intelligent vehicle dispatch and fleet management.

Unlike traditional ride-hailing platforms, our system is designed around the operational needs of autonomous electric fleets, combining passenger convenience with battery-aware vehicle assignments and real-time fleet coordination.

ℹ️
Prototype Notice
Our initial prototype, planned for December 31, 2026, will demonstrate the platform using simulated vehicle and trip data. This first release will not offer actual transportation services. Future commercial deployment will be explored subject to vehicle integration, operational testing, and regulatory approval.

Why we're building this

Our mission is to make autonomous transportation more accessible, reliable, and efficient through intelligent ride-matching technology. Our Platform

A better way to connect people and autonomous vehicles

Our platform brings together a passenger-facing mobile application and a fleet management dashboard to support the next generation of ride-hailing services.

Explore our products

For Passengers

Passengers can request rides, view estimated fares and arrival times, and track assigned vehicles through a simple mobile interface.

For Fleet Operators

Our web-based dashboard provides visibility into vehicle availability, charging status, battery levels, and ongoing trips, helping fleets operate more efficiently.

Behind the scenes, an AI-assisted dispatch system evaluates vehicle proximity, battery levels, driving range, trip distance, and traffic conditions to identify suitable vehicles for each journey.

Key Features

What makes our platform different

Every aspect of the platform is designed around the operational realities of autonomous electric fleets.

🧠

Intelligent Ride Matching

Our AI-assisted matching system evaluates passenger location, vehicle proximity, battery level, estimated driving range, trip distance, traffic conditions, and availability to identify suitable autonomous vehicles.

🔋

Battery-Aware Dispatching

The platform considers each vehicle's estimated remaining driving range and required battery reserve before assigning a trip, helping prevent unsuitable vehicle assignments.

⏱️

Dynamic ETA & Fare Estimation

Passengers can view estimated pickup times, trip durations, and fares based on factors such as travel distance, traffic conditions, and demand.

📍

Ride Booking & Real-Time Updates

Passengers can enter pickup and drop-off locations, request rides, review fare estimates, and follow their assigned vehicle's location and trip status.

📊

Fleet Monitoring & Management

Fleet operators can monitor vehicle locations, battery percentages, charging status, availability, and current trip assignments through a centralized dashboard.

⚡

Charging & Availability Management

Vehicles requiring charging or temporarily unavailable for service can be excluded from ride assignments until they are ready to operate.

What's Next

Our Products

Intelligent mobility, from ride request to fleet management.

We're developing a platform built for autonomous electric ride-hailing. It brings together a straightforward passenger experience and the operational tools needed to coordinate a fleet of self-driving electric vehicles.

ℹ️
Our initial prototype uses simulated vehicle and trip data to demonstrate how these systems work together. It will not provide real transportation services.

Passenger App

A simpler way to plan your next ride.

Our passenger-facing mobile app is designed to make requesting an autonomous ride feel familiar and intuitive. Riders enter their pickup location and destination, review an estimated fare and pickup time, and follow their assigned vehicle as the trip progresses.

  • Ride requests: Enter pickup and drop-off locations and request a ride.
  • Upfront estimates: Review estimated fares, pickup times, and trip durations.
  • Vehicle tracking: View the assigned vehicle's simulated location and trip status.
  • Live updates: See refreshed arrival estimates as conditions change.

What Makes Our Platform Different

Built around how autonomous fleets actually operate

🧠

Intelligent Ride Matching

Rather than assigning the nearest vehicle alone, our AI-assisted dispatch approach considers passenger location, vehicle proximity, availability, trip distance, traffic conditions, and estimated driving range.

🔋

Battery-Aware Dispatch

Before a vehicle is assigned, the system checks whether its estimated range can cover the trip while maintaining a required battery reserve. Vehicles that are charging or unavailable are excluded from matching.

🔗

Connected Passenger & Fleet Experiences

The passenger app and operator dashboard are designed around the same trip and vehicle information, so riders can follow their journey while operators monitor fleet activity.

How a Ride Works

From request to arrival — a preview of the experience.

A step-by-step look at how our platform connects a ride request to a completed journey, using simulated vehicles and data.

Step 1

Request a ride

A passenger enters a pickup point and destination in the mobile app.

Step 2

Find a suitable vehicle

The dispatch system evaluates nearby available vehicles, estimated battery range, trip distance, traffic, and reserve requirements.

Step 3

Review the assignment

The passenger sees an assigned vehicle, estimated pickup time, trip duration, and fare estimate.

Step 4

Follow the trip

The app displays simulated vehicle movement and trip-status updates while the fleet dashboard tracks the assignment.

Step 5

Complete the journey

The simulated trip ends, and the vehicle's availability and battery information are updated for future assignments.
